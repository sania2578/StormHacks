Where You Should Go

    Understanding Canadian Care Options

    Know who to see and what to bring before you leave the house.

    🚨 Emergency Room (ER)

    Open 24/7 for life- or limb-threatening conditions requiring advanced hospital care.

    • Chest pain, stroke signs (FAST)
    • Severe shortness of breath
    • Deep wounds, major fractures

    🏥 Urgent & Primary Care (UPCC)

    Same-day care for non-life-threatening issues that need attention within 12–24 hours.

    • Sprains, small cuts needing stitches
    • High fevers, ear infections
    • Non-emergency injuries

    🩺 Walk-In Clinic / GP

    Best for ongoing or non-urgent general health needs and non-emergency checkups.

    • Chronic issue follow-ups
    • Preventive screenings & checkups
    • Referrals to medical specialists

    💊 Community Pharmacy

    Pharmacists can directly assess and prescribe for minor ailments in many provinces.

    • Uncomplicated UTIs & mild rashes
    • Pink eye & cold sores
    • Emergency medication renewals

    👁️ Optometrist Clinic

    Primary eye doctors equipped with specialized microscope slit lamps.

    • Corneal scratches & pink eye
    • Foreign particle in the eye
    • Sudden floaters or eye irritation

    📞 HealthLink 8-1-1

    Free, 24/7 confidential helpline staffed by Registered Nurses and Pharmacists.

    • Free translation in 130+ languages
    • Unsure where to go after hours
    • Medication questions

    Disclaimer: CareGuide is an informational navigation guide designed for newcomers and community members, not a certified diagnostic medical tool. When in doubt, dial 8-1-1 to speak with a Registered Nurse, or 9-1-1 for emergencies.
